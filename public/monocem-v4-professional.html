Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about microcement — from substrate compatibility to aftercare. Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

General

What is microcement?

+

Microcement is a cement-based decorative coating applied at 2–3mm thickness. It is trowelled by hand over almost any existing surface — floors, walls, bathrooms, kitchens — to create a seamless, polished finish that looks like polished concrete but is far more practical in residential settings.

How thick is microcement?

+

MONOCEM is applied in two to three layers totalling just 2–3mm. This is thin enough to apply directly over existing tiles or flooring without raising the floor level significantly — ideal for renovation projects where height is a concern.

Is microcement waterproof?

+

Yes — when correctly applied and sealed, MONOCEM bathroom and wet-room systems are fully waterproof. We use professional-grade polyurethane topcoats that create an impenetrable barrier rated for constant water exposure, including full submersion in shower trays.

How long does microcement last?

+

Professionally applied and properly maintained microcement can last 20+ years. MONOCEM installations are backed by a 5-year workmanship guarantee. Regular maintenance with a pH-neutral cleaner will preserve the finish and appearance long-term.

Installation

Can microcement be applied over existing tiles?

+

Yes. Providing the existing tiles are sound, firmly fixed, and level, MONOCEM can be applied directly over them. We apply an epoxy primer first to ensure a perfect bond. This eliminates the cost and disruption of tile removal — one of microcement's key advantages for renovation projects.

How long does installation take?

+

Most residential floor projects (up to 40m²) take 2–3 days including preparation, application, and sealing. Bathrooms typically take 2–3 days. Multi-room or large commercial projects are scheduled across 4–7 days depending on area and complexity. We'll give you a precise timeline in your quote.

What substrates can microcement be applied to?

+

MONOCEM can be applied to concrete, cement screed, ceramic and porcelain tiles, wooden floors (with suitable primer), OSB, plasterboard, brick, and stone. The substrate must be structurally sound, clean, and free of movement. We assess the substrate during our free consultation.

Is microcement compatible with underfloor heating?

+

Yes — MONOCEM is an excellent conductor of heat, making it ideal for use with underfloor heating systems. We work closely with UFH installers to ensure the system is properly commissioned before application and that temperature settings are appropriate for the microcement cure cycle.

Maintenance & Aftercare

How do I clean microcement?

+

Day-to-day cleaning requires only a damp mop and pH-neutral floor cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaners (bleach, vinegar, lemon-based products) as these can break down the sealant over time. We provide a full aftercare guide at handover, including recommended cleaning products.

Can microcement be repaired if damaged?

+

Minor surface scratches can often be addressed by re-applying the topcoat sealer. Deeper damage may require a localised patch repair. Unlike tiles, microcement repairs can be blended to be near-invisible. We offer an aftercare and repair service for all MONOCEM installations.

Does microcement need resealing?

+

We recommend re-applying a maintenance coat of topcoat sealer every 3–5 years in residential settings, or annually in high-traffic commercial environments. This is a quick process (usually half a day) that restores the surface to its original condition.

Design & Colour

How many colours are available?

+

MONOCEM's core palette contains 30 standard colours across four collections: Neutrals, Cool Tones, Warm Tones, and Outdoor & Industrial. We can also create completely bespoke colours by mixing our mineral pigments — bring us a RAL code, a paint chip, or a fabric swatch and we'll match it.

Can I see samples before committing?

+

Absolutely. We recommend viewing physical sample boards before selecting your colour and finish, as colours and textures can appear different on screen. We can bring samples to your site consultation, or you can request samples by post. Note that microcement naturally has variation — sample boards give the best indication but each installation is unique.

What finishes are available?

+

We offer three topcoat finishes: matt (no sheen, most natural look), satin (subtle sheen, most popular for floors), and gloss (high shine, great for walls and feature areas). For wet rooms and shower floors, we offer a specialist anti-slip finish that provides grip without compromising aesthetics.
